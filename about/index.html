The company

What Transformativa is

Transformativa LLC is a single company, founded and led by Natalie Dudina. Its core work is enterprise transformation and executive advisory: strategic advisory, interim executive leadership, ERP recovery, and tailored transformation teams for executive teams, private equity-backed businesses, and global enterprises. Alongside it, the company runs two other lines of work: authorship and publishing, and software and technology.
