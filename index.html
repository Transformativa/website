The company

What Transformativa is

Transformativa LLC is a single company, founded and led by Natalie Dudina. Its core work is transformation consulting and strategic advisory. Alongside it, the company runs two other lines of work: authorship and publishing, and software and technology.

Each line has its own name so that customers, readers and partners know what they are dealing with. All three are part of one company, run by one owner, with one set of books.

Structure

How the names fit together

Dellis and SpecialMe are assumed business names of Transformativa LLC. They are not separate companies, partnerships or subsidiaries. Transformativa LLC is the legal company behind all three, and it signs contracts and receives payments for each of them.
