Privacy Policy

Transformativa LLC

Effective Date: March 27, 2024

1. Overview

Transformativa LLC (“Transformativa,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you visit our website.

This Privacy Policy applies to https://gettransformativa.com and its associated pages.

Our approach is simple: we collect only the information necessary to understand how our website is used and to respond to communications you choose to initiate.

We do not use visitor information for advertising, profiling, or marketing surveillance.

2. Information We Collect

Website Usage Statistics

We measure how many unique visitors access our website, which pages they view, how long they spend on each page, how far they scroll, and which website referred them. To count unique visitors without collecting personal information, our site stores a randomly generated identifier in your browser. It is not linked to your name, email address, or any other personal detail.

If you use our Transformation Effectiveness Diagnostic, we record that a diagnostic was started or completed, its mode (quick or full), and the overall score. Your individual answers are calculated in your browser and are not sent to us or stored.

We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, demographic profiles, or other personal information for website analytics. We do not use this information to identify individual visitors.

Information You Voluntarily Provide

If you contact Transformativa by email, through a contact form, or through a scheduling service, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, company, and the contents of your message.

We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry, communicate about potential services, or manage an existing business relationship.

3. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Measure website reach and understand which content is most useful, using aggregate counts.
  • Respond to inquiries and requests.
  • Arrange business conversations or consultations.
  • Maintain necessary business correspondence.
  • Protect the security and functionality of our website.

We do not sell personal information or use visitor data for targeted advertising.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Transformativa does not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking technologies, or behavioral profiling tools.

Our website uses your browser’s local storage to keep the anonymous visitor identifier described above and for basic website functionality. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings, which will cause you to be counted as a new visitor on your next visit.

We do not use website analytics to build individual visitor profiles or track activity across unrelated websites.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website relies on third-party providers for hosting, website infrastructure, usage statistics storage, email communications, and appointment scheduling.

These providers may process limited technical information necessary to deliver their services, such as IP addresses and standard connection information.

When you follow a link to a third-party website or use an external scheduling service, that provider’s privacy practices may apply.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

We may share information with service providers when necessary to operate our website, respond to inquiries, or provide requested services.

We may also disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect our rights and the security of our services.

7. Data Retention

We do not maintain individual visitor profiles through our website analytics.

Aggregate and anonymous usage statistics may be retained for business reporting purposes.

Information voluntarily provided through business communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain business relationships, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through our website and business communications.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may browse our website without providing your name, email address, or other directly identifying information.

If you have contacted us and wish to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you provided, please contact us using the email address below.

We will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended for business and professional audiences and is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through this website.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our website, technology, business practices, or legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
