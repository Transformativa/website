Industry: Global Financial Services

Enterprise Operating Model & Process Transformation

900business processes documented30%operating cost-reduction objective

The Challenge

A global financial services organization sought to reduce operating costs by 30%. Leadership initially explored location consolidation, but analysis indicated that consolidation alone would not deliver the required savings. A more fundamental review of how the organization operated was needed.

Our Approach

We shifted the focus from physical footprint reduction to enterprise process transformation.

Approximately 900 business processes were documented and analyzed, identifying manual activities, redundant steps, and inefficient workflows.

We established a transformation governance and prioritization framework, distinguishing immediate improvement opportunities from longer-term initiatives requiring business process management (BPM) and robotic process automation (RPA).

What Changed

The organization moved from a location-consolidation strategy to a structured enterprise-wide process transformation agenda.

Leadership approved the transformation framework and prioritized roadmap, which subsequently supported adoption of BPM and RPA technologies.

The Challenge

A global industrial enterprise needed to modernize intellectual property operations, reduce fragmentation across legacy systems, and improve the consistency of processes and external legal service management.

Our Approach

Supported enterprise SaaS transformation and associated process redesign, coordinating technology migration, business stakeholders, external partners, and organizational readiness.

The transformation involved redesigning workflows, consolidating external relationships, and enabling employees to adopt new operating practices.

What Changed

More than one million intellectual property assets were migrated to a SaaS-enabled environment.

Outside counsel relationships were consolidated from approximately 250 to 50.

More than 3,500 users globally were enabled to adopt new technology and ways of working.

The organization moved toward a more integrated technology and operating environment, with streamlined external relationships and broader global adoption.
